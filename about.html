About Dirt Roll Offs

Who We Are

Dirt Roll Offs is a locally owned and operated dumpster rental, cleanout, and demolition service based in Highland, NY. We take pride in offering fast, reliable, and affordable solutions for homeowners, contractors, and businesses throughout the Hudson Valley.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: provide dependable service with honest pricing and professional results. Whether you’re tackling a renovation, clearing out a property, or managing a construction site, we’re here to make the process easier.

Why Choose Us

We deliver on time, communicate clearly, and treat every project with care. With flexible scheduling, multiple dumpster sizes, and full-service cleanout and demolition options, we’re equipped to handle jobs of all sizes.

Local & Trusted

As a Hudson Valley business, we understand the needs of our community. We’re committed to providing reliable service that customers can count on — every time.
