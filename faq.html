Frequently Asked Questions

What can I put in the dumpster?

Construction debris, household junk, furniture, yard waste, and more. No hazardous materials.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes — depending on availability. Calling early gives you the best chance.

What areas do you serve?

Highland, New Paltz, Poughkeepsie, Marlboro, Milton, and surrounding Hudson Valley areas.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Standard rental is 7 days, but extensions are available upon request.

Do you help load the dumpster?

Yes — our cleanout service includes full loading and removal.
